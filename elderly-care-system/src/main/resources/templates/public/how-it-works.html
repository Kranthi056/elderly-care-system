How It Works

A simple three-step journey to better elderly care.

1

Create Your Account

Register as a family member or elderly user. Fill in basic details and medical preferences. Caregivers register and wait for verification.

2

Request or Accept Care

Families browse services and submit requests. Administrators match verified caregivers based on skills, location, and availability.

3

Track & Communicate

View schedules, receive care progress reports, message your caregiver, and stay informed every step of the way.
